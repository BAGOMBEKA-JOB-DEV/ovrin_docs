# Provider matrix

Different providers support different capabilities. Ovrin documents this explicitly so users can understand what a provider does well, what it silently ignores, and what must be reviewed.

## Why this matters

A provider may support OCR, image inputs, or PDF rasterization differently depending on the implementation. These differences are part of the integration contract.
