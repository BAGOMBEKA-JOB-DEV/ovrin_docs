# Evaluation and accuracy

Ovrin publishes no accuracy figure that its own evaluation harness cannot reproduce — and today it
publishes none at all.

## v1.0.0 promises the API, not accuracy

Ovrin is at v1.0.0, and that version number is a promise about compatibility: the API will not break
without a v2. It is deliberately silent about how accurate the library is, because that has not been
measured. The two claims are kept apart on purpose.

## Where evaluation stands

- **The corpus is synthetic.** `eval/corpus/` holds 25 documents, five in each of five categories —
  invoices, receipts, forms, statements and identity documents — and every one was generated.
- **No run has been committed.** The only report is `no-run`: zeros from the report renderer over an
  empty set of results, not a measurement.
- **Confidence is not calibrated.** It is a ranking signal until there is a real corpus to calibrate it
  against.

## Why synthetic documents are not enough

A generated corpus measures the generator as much as the extractor. It lets the harness run, and lets
the harness itself be tested, but a figure computed on it would say little about the documents you care
about.

## What would change this

A corpus of real, redistributable documents in every category, committed reports across at least two
provider generations, and a calibration run. Those are the open items on the
[roadmap](/community/roadmap).
