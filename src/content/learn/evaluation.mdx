# Evaluation and accuracy

The upstream Ovrin project is explicit that no accuracy claim is published unless it is reproduced by the evaluation harness. It also states that real-world corpus work is still necessary before confidence and accuracy claims can be trusted.

## Why evaluation matters

To justify a document-extraction product, you need evidence that it works on the kinds of documents you care about. Synthetic corpora are useful, but they do not replace real data.

## Current stage

The project has evaluation infrastructure, but it remains careful about claiming a general accuracy number before real corpus data is in place.
